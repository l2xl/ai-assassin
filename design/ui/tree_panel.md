# The tree panel is the outline of the DAG

The DAG of syngate items is rendered as a tree: an item with several parents appears once under each of them, every row the same item. The tree doubles as the table of contents of the content panel. A row is the collapse mark or the leaf dot, the common item label, the chat state mark, the title and, at the right edge, the `⋯` menu (`⇑ Add parent`, `+ Add child`, `⊖ Unlink from P`, `✕ Delete item`); nothing else on the row acts in one click.

- **Drag and drop.** A row is dragged by its handle (icon and UID). The top and bottom quarters of a target row mean before and after it, the middle means under it; after an unfolded branch means first among its children; the root accepts only under. Holding the drop over a folded branch unfolds it after a moment; Ctrl at the drop adds the target as one more parent instead of moving. Alt+Shift+arrows are the keyboard twin. An item no root reaches is listed after the roots; a parent link that closes a cycle is shown once, marked `(cycle)`, and unfolds no further. A drop takes a free `order` key between the item's new neighbours, else the family is renumbered.
- **Clicks.** A single click on a row selects it without activating or scrolling to its block, an editor open elsewhere staying open; a double click activates the row's block and scrolls to it unless it is already in view. The splitter resizes the outline by dragging within its width limits, the width kept for the browser session.
- **Menu.** `Add parent` is not offered on a root; it opens a search row under the item taking the UID of an item that is not yet its parent and would not close a cycle, adding one more parent, never replacing one. `Add child` opens the new-UID row under the item with a UID suggested from its siblings, Enter creating the item and opening its editor, Esc cancelling. `Unlink from P` is offered only when the item has several parents and drops the link to the parent the row sits under, after a confirmation when the item is reviewed, since its parents are under the stamp. `Delete item` only on a childless item, after the confirm `Delete UID and its file PATH? This cannot be undone.`; the menu opens with its first entry focused, arrows move along it, Esc returns to the `⋯` button.
- **Filter.** The header's filter field (`/` focuses it; placeholder `Filter by UID or text (press /)`) and the three status chips (`N failed`, `N not run`, `N passed`, a chosen chip showing ` ×`) narrow both panes; the outline unfolds to every match and keeps an ancestor for a matching descendant; a move is refused while a filter is on.
- **Chat state.** The row carries a mark after the label only while the item has a chat: the mark of the chat's state; clicking it activates the block and shows the chat.
- **Keyboard.** Up and Down, PageUp and PageDown, Home and End move the selection in the pane holding the focus (the tree walks the folded outline, the document every item); Right and Left unfold and fold a row; Enter opens the editor; Esc is the done mark; Ctrl/⌘+Enter sends — queues while a turn runs — and Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Enter sends now, stopping the running turn; Ctrl/⌘+. stops the turn; `m` on a focused block opens its row menu, `s` its status popup; `?` lists the keys. Tab moves between the filter, the selected row with its tools and the selected block: every border-mounted control and tool is a button in the Tab order, the reply line joining it only with the open chat or the editor.
